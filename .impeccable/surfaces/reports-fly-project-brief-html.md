## Design judgment

The workshop challenger is competitive in clarity but its primary-color exercises add a metaphor the report does not need. Its discipline is retained as a clear distinction between input, circuit, and intervention. The festival, cloud-quarry, cyclorama, spectral rail, and film-bench challengers lose audience identification and clarity for this scientific reading task. Their useful disciplines are respectively a single decisive focal field, explicit component boundaries, labeled discrete states, redundant state signals, and keeping deferred work accessible. These are implemented as hierarchy, readable diagram compartments, native radio controls, textual state output, and visible future-work notes.
